Our Mission & Vision

Building India's official unified sports digitization infrastructure under the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports.

Championing Grassroots

SportsBharat was established to act as a robust catalyst for discovering, nurturing, and managing sports talents across all 28 Indian States and 8 Union Territories.

By integrating advanced analytics dashboards, QR pass verification attendance registries, and tamper-proof cryptographic validated digital certificates, we provide athletes with a frictionless professional ecosystem to excel globally.
