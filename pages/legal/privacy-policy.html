Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026  |  Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India

SportsBharat ("the Portal") is an initiative of the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India, operated under the National Digital Sports Mission. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you register or use the Portal:

  • Identity data: full name, date of birth, gender
  • Contact data: email address, phone number, state/district
  • Sports data: sport discipline, athlete ID, competition history, achievements
  • Authentication data: encrypted password (never stored in plaintext)
  • Usage data: pages visited, actions taken, browser and device type

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Portal and its features
  • To verify athlete identity and issue tamper-proof digital certificates
  • To facilitate tournament registration and competition management
  • To send transactional notifications relevant to your registered activities
  • To comply with legal obligations and government audit requirements
  • To improve services through aggregated, anonymised analytics

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Processing is carried out on the basis of (a) your consent, (b) performance of a contract to provide services requested, and (c) compliance with legal obligations under Indian law.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared with:

  • State Sports Authorities and National Federations for competition administration
  • Authorised government agencies for verification and audit purposes
  • Technology service providers bound by data-processing agreements

5. Data Security

The Portal employs industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, two-step verification, and regular VAPT audits by CERT-In empanelled agencies.

6. Your Rights

Under the DPDPA 2023 you have the right to: access your personal data, correct inaccuracies, withdraw consent, and request erasure subject to legal retention requirements. Contact our Grievance Officer to exercise these rights.

7. Cookies

We use essential session cookies required for authentication and theme preference. No third-party advertising cookies are set. You may disable cookies in your browser, but certain features may not function.

8. Grievance Officer

In accordance with the IT Act 2000 and DPDPA 2023, the Grievance Officer for SportsBharat is:
Director (Digital Initiatives)
Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports
Shastri Bhawan, New Delhi – 110001
Email: grievance@sports.gov.in

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will be notified via email to registered users. Continued use of the Portal after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related queries: support@sports.gov.in or visit Contact Us.
