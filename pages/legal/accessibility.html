Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: 1 June 2026  |  Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India

SportsBharat is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including persons with disabilities, in compliance with the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW) 3.0, WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 (RPWD Act).

Accessibility Features on This Portal

Font Size Controls Use A− / A / A+ buttons in the top bar to decrease, reset, or increase text size across the entire portal.
High Contrast Mode Toggle the 👁️ High Contrast button to switch to a black-and-yellow high-contrast theme that meets WCAG contrast requirements for low-vision users.
Bilingual Support The portal is available in English and Hindi. Use EN / हिन्दी buttons to switch languages. All core content and labels translate accordingly.
Dark / Light Theme Toggle between light and dark colour schemes using the 🌙 button. Preference is saved across sessions.
Skip Navigation A "Skip to main content" link is available at the top of every page for keyboard users to bypass repeated navigation.
Keyboard Navigation All interactive elements (buttons, links, form fields) are reachable and operable by keyboard. Focus indicators are clearly visible.
Screen Reader Support Pages use semantic HTML5 landmarks (header, nav, main, footer), ARIA labels, and descriptive alt text on all images.
Form Accessibility All form fields have associated labels. Error messages are programmatically associated with inputs. Required fields are marked.

Conformance Status

SportsBharat partially conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. "Partially conforms" means that some content does not yet fully meet the standard. Known limitations are listed below.

Known Limitations

  • The interactive map on the Infrastructure page relies on a third-party library (Leaflet/OpenStreetMap). Map content may not be fully navigable by keyboard. A text-based facility list is provided as an accessible alternative.
  • Some news article images sourced from external APIs may lack descriptive captions beyond the article title. We apply fallback branded placeholder images when external sources are unavailable.
  • PDF documents linked from this portal may not be fully accessible. We are progressively remediating these.

Feedback and Contact

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of SportsBharat. If you encounter barriers or require content in an alternative format, please contact us:

  • Email: accessibility@sports.gov.in
  • Phone: +91 11 2338 1234 (Mon–Fri, 9:00–17:30 IST)
  • Online: Contact Form

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 2 working days.

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India at ccdisabilities.nic.in.

Technical Specifications

This portal relies on the following technologies: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES2020). Compatibility is tested with Chrome, Firefox, and Edge, and with NVDA and VoiceOver screen readers.
